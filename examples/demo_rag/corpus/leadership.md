# Acme Corp — Leadership

Jane Smith is CEO and co-founder. She founded Acme in 2018 after seven years leading
operations at a national freight carrier.

Raj Patel is CTO. He joined Acme in 2020 from a fintech startup where he led the
infrastructure organization.

Maria Chen is VP of Sales. She joined in 2023 and grew the field sales team from 4 to 18
quota-carrying reps in her first 18 months.

The board has five seats: two founder seats, two investor seats (Lightning Ventures, Sigma
Partners), and one independent seat held by a former public-company CFO.
