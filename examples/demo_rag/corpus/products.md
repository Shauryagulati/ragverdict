# Acme Corp — Products

Acme's flagship product, RouteOps, is a logistics scheduling platform used by mid-market
freight carriers across North America. RouteOps integrates with major TMS systems and was
deployed at 84 customer sites by the end of Q1 2025.

A secondary product, InventoryMind, handles SKU-level inventory reconciliation for
retailers. InventoryMind launched in 2024 and grew to 12 paying customers in its first
nine months.

Both products are offered as multi-tenant SaaS with on-prem deployment available for
enterprise customers.
