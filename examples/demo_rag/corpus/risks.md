# Acme Corp — Risk Factors

Customer concentration: The top three customers accounted for 47% of revenue in Q1 2025,
exposing Acme to material loss if any single relationship deteriorated.

Foreign exchange: Approximately 18% of revenue is invoiced in EUR. A sustained 10% adverse
move would reduce reported revenue by roughly $1M annually at current run-rate.

Regulatory: Pending EU AI Act provisions could classify InventoryMind's price-optimization
features as "high-risk", triggering additional compliance costs estimated at $200K in the
first year.

Talent: Engineering attrition was 14% in 2024, above the industry median. Acme has hired
two senior engineers in Q1 2025 to backfill.
