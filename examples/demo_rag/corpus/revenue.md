# Acme Corp — Revenue

Acme reported $5.2M in Q1 2025 revenue, a 38% increase year over year. Annual recurring
revenue at the end of Q1 stood at $21M.

Revenue by segment in Q1 2025:
- Subscription: $4.1M
- Professional services: $0.7M
- Hardware passthrough: $0.4M

Bookings outpaced revenue by 22% in the quarter, suggesting continued growth into Q2 2025.
